Current rules

Every Topic stays open at least its minimum life, then closes once it has been quiet for its quiet time. It passes if enough Readers voted (turnout) and enough votes were in favour (approval). Only turnout and approval can change, by a Rules vote, 5 points at a time and never past the limits below.
