Current Rules

A Conversation closes once its Question has been attached for its Minimum Life and it has then gone without new activity for its Seasoning Time. The Question passes if enough Contactors Voted (the Threshold) and enough of the Votes were in favor (the Approval). A Conversation without a Question stays open for Discussion.

Each Vote can move a number by at most 5% of its current value, and always by at least 1 point, in whole percentages. Every Threshold and Approval always stays between 9% and 100%.

Contactors are the Members who have touched a Conversation: opened it, posted a Comment or Voted. Threshold = Voters ÷ Contactors. Approval = Votes in favor ÷ all Votes; in the Forum Committee, Abstain counts in favor.
