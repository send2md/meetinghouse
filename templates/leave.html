Leave Meetinghouse

You can leave at any time. This is what happens:

  • Your Comments and Votes come off every open Conversation, with no note naming you. Seasoning Time starts over in each one where you had a Comment or a Vote, so everyone can see the change.
  • A Conversation you started, or attached the Question to, goes on: its Title, opening words and Question stay, under your name.
  • Your Comments and Votes in closed Conversations stay on the record under your name, because the Meeting's decisions rest on them. A plain “left Meetinghouse” picture takes the place of your photo.
  • Your email address, photo and passkeys are deleted, and you can't log in again.
  • A Jury Conversation about you, if there is one, goes on to its result.
  • You can come back later by joining again, with a new ID check.
